Featured Project · Jul 2026 — Aug 2026
Biomedical Text Classification with LoRA
Implemented LoRA from scratch in PyTorch to fine-tune RoBERTa-base on PubMed-RCT abstracts, then benchmarked it against full fine-tuning across five ranks and two scaling rules. Traced a training collapse in the alpha/√r runs at rank 16 and above to a scaling factor that grew with rank.
